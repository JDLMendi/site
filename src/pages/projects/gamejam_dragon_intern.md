During the 2024 internship for the CTL, I've joined up with one of the interns in the annual Game Jam hosted by Pirate Coves.

We've thought up a simple game where you play as an Apprentice- Sorry, Intern for a dragon that dwells under a magical city where you have to complete side quests to collect ingredients to craft special potions to help you through the following day. It was supposed to be under some dark themes but in the end we somehow created a bit of a humourous game instead.

I've focused on the way how we could create potions and part of the story, bringing ideas for dialogues and ways to progress the story.
